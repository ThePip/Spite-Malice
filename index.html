    How to Play

    Spite & Malice

    The Goal
    🏆 Empty your Goal stack before your opponent does. The top card of your Goal can be played to the Centre ▶ whenever it fits — that's your main path to winning.
    Centre ▶ Stacks
    ⬆️ Built low to high, one rank at a time. Suit doesn't matter.
    ✅ When a stack reaches its highest card it is cleared and shuffled back into the draw deck.
    Wilds
    🃏 A wild can stand in for any card on a Centre ▶ stack.
    ⚠️ You cannot end your turn with a wild on top of a Centre ▶ stack — cover it with a real card first.
    Your Turn
    🤚 Play cards from your hand, your Goal stack, or the top card of any of your Discard ▽ stacks.
    🔒 Only the top card of each Discard ▽ stack can be played.
    ▽ Your turn must end by discarding one card from your hand to one of your Discard ▽ stacks — no other way to end it.
    Keep your Discard ▽ stacks tidy — burying a card you need means waiting until it's back on top.
